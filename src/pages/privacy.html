Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Draft for legal review. Written to match how the site works today. Have a lawyer check it against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 before relying on it.

Who we are

Kukreja's Wastec Bio-Gas Private Limited ("Wastec"), registered at AIC-BARC, 1st Floor, DAE Con, Anushaktinagar, Mumbai 400094, CIN U38210MH2025PTC438888.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: name, phone, email, organisation type, location, waste type and quantity, and your message.
  • Accounts: name, organisation, phone, email and a password (stored only as a hash by our sign-in provider).
  • Plant data: sensor readings, alerts, service requests, contracts and invoices for plants on your account.
  • Technical data: basic request logs kept by our hosting and database providers for security.

Why we use it

To reply to enquiries, quote and supply equipment, run your account and dashboard, raise alerts, send the alert emails you ask for, dispatch technicians, invoice you, and keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces load from Google's servers.
  • Razorpay: payments, if you subscribe. We do not store card details.
  • Anthropic: if you use the plant assistant, your question and that plant's recent readings are sent to generate the answer.
  • An email delivery provider: to send alert emails you switch on.

Some of these providers process data outside India.

Who can see your plant data

Users in your organisation and authorised Wastec staff. Other customers cannot. Plant data belongs to you. We use it to provide the service and, in aggregate and without identifying you, to improve alert limits.

How long we keep it

Enquiries for up to 24 months. Account and plant data while your account is active, and afterwards for the period the law requires for invoices. You can ask us to delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent for alert emails at any time in the app, and raise a complaint with us through the contact page. If we do not resolve it, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Access is restricted by organisation at the database level, devices authenticate with individual keys, and connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above.
