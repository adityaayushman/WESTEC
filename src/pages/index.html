Questions

Common questions

What waste can go into a biogas plant?

Cattle dung, poultry litter, pig manure, kitchen and food waste, and vegetable or fruit waste all work. Each gives a different yield and needs a different design, which is why the first step is knowing your feedstock.

Can you service a plant someone else built?

Yes. Many faults come from the same few causes whatever the make: gas leaks, blocked pipes, scrubber saturation, mixer failure and feed problems.

What is monitoring, and do I need it?

Sensors measure the plant and send readings to a dashboard. You do not need it to run a plant, but it shows a problem days before it becomes a shutdown, and it is most useful for plants where nobody checks daily.

Is biogas safe?

Yes when the plant is built and maintained properly. Biogas is flammable and H₂S is toxic, so gas-tightness, ventilation, flame arrestors and trained operators matter. We do not give repair instructions for gas-handling parts online. Call us.

Are there subsidies?

Central and state schemes for biogas and bio-CNG exist and change often. Tell us your location and use, and we will point you to the current scheme to check.
