Common faults

What usually goes wrong

Diagnosis needs a person on site, but these are the usual suspects.

SymptomCommon causes
Gas production fallsCold slurry, under-feeding, sudden change of feed, acidification, a leak
Flame weak or yellowLow methane, water in the pipe, blocked burner, low pressure
Rotten-egg smellH₂S breakthrough, exhausted scrubber media, a gas leak
Foam or crust in the tankOverfeeding, fats and oils, poor mixing
Engine wearing fastUntreated H₂S and moisture in the gas

If you smell gas, put out flames, ventilate, keep away from switches and call us or the emergency services. Do not attempt repairs on gas lines yourself.
