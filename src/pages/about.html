About

A biogas company focused on uptime

Wastec supplies biogas machinery and maintains biogas plants. Our work starts where many projects stop: after commissioning, when a plant has to produce every day.

Kukreja's Wastec Bio-Gas Private Limited is a private company registered in Mumbai in January 2025, working in the treatment of non-hazardous waste through biogas. We trade machinery and maintain a range of biogas equipment, and we are building monitoring tools so plant owners can see what their plant is doing.

We think the industry loses most value to avoidable breakdowns. A simple sensor and a timely alert often cost less than one lost week of gas.
